### 🧠 About Me

I'm a dual-degree CS + EE grad who lives at the intersection of hardware and software. I design digital circuits in Verilog, build embedded systems on Raspberry Pis, and train neural networks — sometimes all in the same week. I spent three years as a teaching assistant and built real production tooling along the way. Now I'm looking to bring that range into industry.

- 🎥 Built **[Grappler Camera System](https://github.com/peejerator/grappler-camera)** — a distributed AI-powered multi-camera tracking platform for combat sports using YOLO11n-pose on Raspberry Pi 5 nodes with PCA9685 servo control
- ⚡ Designed a **[Power-Aware Systolic MAC Array](https://github.com/peejerator/systolic-mac-array)** on Xilinx Zynq UltraScale+ — 10× switching activity reduction, timed at 100 MHz
- 🛠️ Authored a **C++ Autograding Framework** used in production each semester — libclang AST analysis, LLM-assisted review, batch interactive grading
- 🎓 Former Teaching Assistant for COP 3014, COP 3363, COP 3330, CGS 2100 — ~100+ students/semester over 3 years
- 📍 Tampa, FL
